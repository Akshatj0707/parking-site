How it works

  1. Come to the lot at your booked time. The entry QR code starts working a few minutes before it begins.
  2. At the gate, show the entry QR to the staff. They scan it with their phone and send you to your bay. It works once.
  3. When you leave, show the exit QR. If you leave within the booked time, that is all.
  4. Need more time? While you are parked, and before your booked time ends, add hours in the “Need more time?” box on this page, if the bay is free. If you stay past the end without adding time, every extra hour is charged at your booking’s hourly rate (the first 10 minutes are free). You pay it here on your phone, and then you can drive out.

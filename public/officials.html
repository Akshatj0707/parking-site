What an official can do

A government official signs in on the normal Sign in page with the email and password you create here. They see all the data: every booking, every customer, all payments and numbers, and they can download the spreadsheet. They cannot cancel or refund a booking, change any setting, or create other logins. Only you can do that.

Nobody can create an official login by signing up. Customers sign up by themselves and are always customers.

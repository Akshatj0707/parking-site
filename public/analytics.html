    How to read this page

    • Money collected counts the payments received on that day, in rupees, on the lot's clock. A booking that was cancelled and refunded is taken out.
    • Vehicles parked is worked out from bookings, not from sensors: a vehicle counts for the part of the hour that its paid booking covers. For a period of several days it is the average for one day.
    • Arrivals are paid bookings that start in that hour (an average per day for a period of several days).
    • The busiest hour is shown in yellow with an outline. The same numbers are in the table below each chart.

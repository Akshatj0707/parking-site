Press the button after the site was put online or moved to a new host, or after you add the chat key.

How to read this page

  • In use now means a paid booking is running at this moment. It is based on bookings, not on sensors, so it cannot tell whether the vehicle has actually arrived.
  • Booked later today is a paid booking that starts after now.
  • Payment in progress is a bay held for someone who is paying right now. It is released after {{HOLD_MINUTES}} minutes if they do not finish.
  • Money figures count payments received today, in rupees. A booking that is cancelled and refunded is taken out of them.
  • To cancel a booking and give the money back, use Cancel and refund a booking at the top of the page.

        How to read this page

        • Money collected is the payments received in that period, in rupees, on the lot’s clock. A booking that was cancelled and refunded is taken out, and shown under Refunded.
        • In use now is based on paid bookings, not on sensors, so it cannot tell whether a vehicle has really arrived.
        • Cancelled or refunded is the share of all booked bays that were later cancelled by you or refunded.
        • Repeat customers are signed-in customers with two or more paid bookings.

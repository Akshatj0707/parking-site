Questions

Good to know.

Do I need an account?

No. You book with your vehicle number and pay online. To see a booking again, open Find my booking and type the same number.

How long is my bay held while I pay?

{{HOLD_MINUTES}} minutes from the moment you press pay. If the time runs out, the bay goes back on sale and you can start again.

How do weekday and weekend rates work?

Weekday rates apply on {{WEEKDAY_TEXT}} and weekend rates on {{WEEKEND_TEXT}}. The rate depends on the day your booking starts, and it applies to every hour of that booking.

What if I arrive late?

Your bay stays reserved for the whole time you booked, even if you arrive late. The booking ends at the end time you chose.

Can I stay longer, or cancel?

To stay longer, make a second booking that starts when your first one ends. You can pick the same bay if it is free. Cancelling is not possible on the website yet. {{SUPPORT_LINE}} The cancellation and refund policy says what we do.

My payment went through but I saw an error. What now?

Please do not pay twice. We check every payment with Razorpay, and a payment that cannot become a booking is refunded automatically, usually within 5 to 10 working days. If something still looks wrong, contact us with your vehicle number. {{SUPPORT_LINE}}

Who can see my booking?

Anyone who types in your vehicle number can see its bay and booking time in Find my booking. We never show names, phone numbers or payment details there.

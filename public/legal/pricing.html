How the price is worked out

  • You choose a whole number of hours, from {{HOURS_MIN}} to {{HOURS_MAX}}. The price is the hourly rate times the hours. For example, a car for 3 hours on a weekday is 3 × ₹{{RATE_CAR_WEEKDAY}}.
  • The rate depends on the day your booking starts. It applies to every hour of that booking.
  • There is no booking fee, and nothing is added at checkout. What you see before you pay is what you pay.

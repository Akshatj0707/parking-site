Who this is about

{{BUSINESS_NAME}} (“we”) runs this website. This page explains what we keep when you book a bay, why we keep it, and who else can see it.

What we keep

  • Your booking: the vehicle number and vehicle type, the bay, the date, start time and number of hours, the price, and a booking reference.
  • Your payment record: the Razorpay payment ID and order ID, the amount, and whether the payment was paid or refunded.

This website does not ask for your name, phone number or email address.

When you pay, Razorpay collects what it needs to take the payment, such as your phone number or email address and your card, UPI or bank details. Razorpay’s own privacy policy applies to that. Our website never sees or keeps your card, UPI or bank details. Details about a payment, including the contact details you gave Razorpay, may be visible to us in our Razorpay account. We use them only to deal with that payment, for example a refund or a question about it.

What we use it for

  • To give you the bay you booked, and to show it to you when you type your vehicle number into Find my booking.
  • To take your payment and, when needed, to refund it.
  • To answer your questions and to sort out problems or disputes about a booking or a payment.
  • To keep this website safe and working, and to keep the records that the law asks us to keep.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else can see it

  • Anyone who knows your vehicle number can see, in Find my booking, the bay and the booking time of that vehicle until the booking has ended. No names, phone numbers or payment details are shown there.
  • Razorpay processes your payment.
  • Our hosting provider stores the bookings on its server for us.
    • {{CHAT_SHARING}}
  • Our staff can look up a booking by vehicle number, and cancel and refund it, behind a password.
  • We share information with the authorities only when the law requires it.

On your device

This website does not set cookies and has no advertising or analytics. To save you typing, it remembers the last vehicle number you used in your own browser, and it keeps a booking that is in progress in your browser while you pay. These stay on your device. Razorpay’s payment window may use its own cookies: see Razorpay’s privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Bookings that were never paid are deleted automatically after about a day. Paid and refunded bookings, and their payment records, are kept for as long as we need them for our accounts, for refunds and disputes, and as the law requires.

Security

This website uses HTTPS, and the staff area is behind a password. No system is perfectly secure, but we take care to protect the information we hold.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about a booking, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We will deal with your request, but we may have to keep some records, such as payment records that the law requires. {{SUPPORT_LINE}}

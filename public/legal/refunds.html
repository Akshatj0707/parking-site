Cancelling a booking

You cannot cancel on the website yet. To cancel, contact us as soon as you can and give your vehicle number or booking reference. {{SUPPORT_LINE}}

  • Before your booking starts: we cancel it and refund the full amount.
  • After your booking has started: the bay was held for you, so we do not normally refund. If we could not give you the bay you paid for, we do.

A booking that has already ended cannot be cancelled.

If you paid but did not get the bay

Sometimes a payment arrives after the hold has ended and the bay is no longer free, for example when you pay just as the {{HOLD_MINUTES}} minutes run out. We check every payment with Razorpay. When a payment cannot become a booking, we send the full amount back automatically. You do not need to do anything, and please do not pay a second time.

If we cancel

If we have to cancel your booking, for example because the lot is closed or a bay cannot be used, we refund the full amount.

How refunds are paid

  • A refund is for the full amount you paid.
  • It goes back to the payment method you used: the same card, UPI ID, bank account or wallet.
  • Razorpay says refunds take 5 to 10 working days to show up, depending on your bank or card issuer. Once we have sent a refund we cannot make it faster, but your bank can tell you where it is.

If something looks wrong

If money left your account and you did not get a confirmation, or a refund has not arrived after 10 working days, contact us. Give your vehicle number and, if you have it, the payment ID that Razorpay showed you (it starts with “pay_”). {{SUPPORT_LINE}}
